3,308 indicator-and-stock combinations were tested. 10 scored 8 or above.

10 of last week's 12 survivors survived again. Repeating twice is weak evidence, not strong: with this many combinations tested, some will repeat by chance alone. These two runs are only 3 days apart, so they were scored on almost the same data. A combination repeating across windows that overlap this heavily is close to arithmetic rather than evidence, and this comparison should not be read as one until the runs are a full week apart.
