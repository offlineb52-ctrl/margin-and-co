What you get

  • The live paper portfolio, in detail. Open positions, orders committed for the next session before they fill, and every closed trade with the reason it was taken.
  • Research updates as they are produced. What the published rules flagged, what it cost to act on them, and how that compares to doing nothing.
  • The weekly report, in full, when it is published.

What you will never get

No stock tips. Not now, not for money. This site publishes what a mechanical, fully-disclosed rule did — not what you should buy. Nothing here is a recommendation, a price target, or a forecast.

That is a legal line as well as an editorial one. In the UK, advising on investments as a business requires FCA authorisation, and I do not have it — I am a sixth-form student running a research project. Anyone selling you daily picks without that authorisation is either exempt in a way they should be able to explain, or is breaking the law.

How sign-in works

You enter your email address and a single-use link arrives. Clicking it signs you in for thirty days. The link expires in fifteen minutes and stops working once used.

There is no password because there is nothing to store. The most common way accounts are compromised is a password reused from a site that got breached — a risk that cannot exist here. Session identifiers are held hashed, so even a copy of the database would not let anyone sign in as you.

What is stored about you

Your email address, when you joined, and when you last signed in. No name, no location, no tracking, no advertising identifiers. The privacy policy is specific about this, and asking for your account to be deleted takes one email and no explanation.
