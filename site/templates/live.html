Why long only

The research tests every indicator long and short, because that is how the rule is defined. This book trades the long leg only. Shorting incurs a stock borrow fee that varies by name and by day, and this project does not model it — so a live short book would report a return nobody could actually have earned.

What would falsify this

If the portfolio finishes behind buy & hold on a risk-adjusted basis over a full year of forward recording, the honest conclusion is that the Week 1 result was noise. That conclusion will be published here in the same place as everything else.

Audit it. The complete order book is published as raw data: every fill, every daily mark, and the derived figures shown on this page. Positions are never stored — they are replayed from the fills, so the trade log is the only source of truth.
